How to Contribute

This directory is 100% open-source and maintained by the developer community. If you know of an official government API, open dataset, or verified portal, follow these quick steps:

1

Pick the Right Category

Identify which of the 8 categories best suits the API (Identity, Finance, Logistics, Agriculture, OGD, Health, Weather, or Legal).

2

Verify the Official Link

Ensure the URL points to an official government domain (e.g. .gov.in, .nic.in, .org.in) or verified national body.

3

Format Your Table Row
